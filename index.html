A peaceful garden retreat in Jaipur

Baisa Home Stay is a calm retreat on Jaipur Ring Road, just 10 minutes from Chokhi Dhani. Our spacious, well-lit rooms come with dedicated work desks, a shared balcony and views over a beautiful garden — a restful base for families, couples, business travellers and long stays.

Some rooms are air-conditioned, and breakfast and extra mattresses are available on request. Food delivery, Blinkit and cabs are all easy to find nearby. Whether you are here for a night or a whole season, you are family at Baisa.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is Baisa Home Stay located?

Baisa Home Stay is at ID/130, 14 Ring Road, near Vatika Road, Beelwa, Sukhdeopura Nohara, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302029 — a garden retreat on Jaipur Ring Road in the Vatika–Beelwa area of south Jaipur.

How far is Baisa from Chokhi Dhani?

About 10 minutes by road — roughly 6 km — which makes Baisa a handy base for a visit to Chokhi Dhani.

How many rooms does Baisa have, and are they air-conditioned?

Baisa has six rooms: three air-conditioned and three non-AC. Every room has a work desk, and guests share the balcony and garden.

Do you offer long-term or monthly stays in Jaipur?

Yes. Rooms are available for short stays and for long-term rental, with monthly rates for longer stays. Message or call us with your dates and we will share a quote.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is after 2:00 PM and check-out is before 10:00 AM. We offer self check-in, with our building staff to let you in.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking is available on the premises.

Is breakfast available?

Breakfast and extra mattresses are available on request. Food delivery, Blinkit and cabs are also easy to find nearby.

How do I book a room, and what are the rates?

Book rooms on Airbnb, or message or call us on +91 82094 30330 or +91 94604 91850. Rates are shared on request — tell us your dates and number of guests and we usually reply within an hour.

Who is Baisa suitable for?

Families, couples, business travellers and long-stay guests. The rooms are spacious and well lit, with work desks, a shared balcony and a quiet garden.

How far is Baisa from Jaipur airport and railway station?

By road, Baisa is roughly 17 km from Jaipur International Airport and about 23 km from Jaipur Junction railway station. Cabs are easy to find nearby.

Do you have a party hall?

Yes — Baisa Party Hall is a private indoor hall on the lower level for birthdays, engagements and ring ceremonies, kitty parties, anniversaries, family get-togethers and small functions. Message us your date and guest count on WhatsApp, or read more on our Party Hall page.
