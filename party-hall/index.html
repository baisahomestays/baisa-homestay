The Hall

An indoor hall made for celebrations

Baisa Party Hall is a private indoor hall on the lower level of Baisa Home Stay on Jaipur Ring Road, about 10 minutes from Chokhi Dhani. It is a spacious, well-ventilated hall with an elegant stage and décor and comfortable banquet-style seating — set up and ready for your celebration.

Parking is available on the premises, and catering and décor assistance can be arranged on request. Guests travelling for your event can also enquire about our six homestay rooms.

Good to know

Party hall: frequently asked questions

What events can I host at Baisa Party Hall?

Birthdays, engagements and ring ceremonies, kitty parties, anniversaries, family get-togethers and other small functions.

Where is Baisa Party Hall?

On the lower level of Baisa Home Stay, ID/130, 14 Ring Road, near Vatika Road, Beelwa, Jaipur 302029 — about 10 minutes from Chokhi Dhani.

What does the hall offer?

A spacious, well-ventilated indoor hall with an elegant stage and décor, comfortable banquet-style seating and parking on the premises. Catering and décor assistance are available on request.

How do I check availability and pricing?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 82094 30330 or call, with your date, occasion and approximate guest count. We usually reply within an hour, and pricing is shared when you enquire.

Is parking available for guests?

Yes, parking is available on the premises.

Can out-of-town guests stay at Baisa?

Yes. The hall is part of the same property as our six-room homestay (three AC, three non-AC), so guests travelling for your event can also enquire about rooms. Rooms can be booked on Airbnb or on WhatsApp.
